About her

Stories first, then the rest of the world.

An English Literature graduate with a quiet devotion to books, pencils and green things.

Suraia has studied English Literature and keeps returning to it: to the page, to the sentence that stops you, to the worlds that open between two covers. When she is not reading she is drawing and sketching, turning what she notices into line and shade.

She is happiest among trees. Greenery steadies her, and she carries that stillness into everything she makes and loves.

    The literary soul

    A life lived between the lines.

    Literature taught her to look closely, to listen for what is unsaid, and to find company in a sentence.
